Our Mission

The PHive is SCOPH's digital knowledge hub dedicated to making public health accessible, engaging, and inspiring for students and communities. We bridge the gap between research and practice by providing reliable information, educational resources, opportunities, and a platform to showcase the impact of SCOPH initiatives.

Our Vision

To cultivate a thriving public health community where knowledge sparks action and every member contributes to healthier communities — one cell of the hive at a time.

About the PHive

Built by SCOPH, for every public health mind at NUSMSA.

The PHive is SCOPH's digital knowledge hub — the place where public health minds connect, learn, and create impact. We exist to make public health accessible, engaging, and inspiring for students and the communities we serve, by bridging the gap between research and practice with reliable information, educational resources, real opportunities, and a stage for the work our members do.

Every section of this site is a cell in the same hive: news keeps you current, research and articles deepen your understanding, and opportunities turn that understanding into action.
